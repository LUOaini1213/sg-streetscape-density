Which street feels less crowded?

Pairwise-comparison instrument for the sg-streetscape-density project. Independent personal project, not affiliated with the Singapore-ETH Centre or the NUS Urban Analytics Lab.

Demo only. This page sends nothing anywhere and no study is running. Your choices stay in this browser tab. At the end you can see a Bradley–Terry ranking computed in the page and download your own answers as JSON. A real pilot would need ethics approval first (see the protocol).

You will see 20 pairs of street scenes from Singapore (KartaView photos, CC BY-SA 4.0). For each pair, click the scene that feels less crowded. Go with your first impression.

Keyboard: left arrow = left scene, right arrow = right scene.
